# eHome EMS b14 — design QA

## Scope and evidence

User screenshots: failed Conso Maison, inconsistent entity-card alignment, unwanted curves, cumulative energy shown as daily cost, rejected portable-battery stock, integer percentages and a matching Today weather card.

Desktop 1440×1600 and mobile 390×844 screenshots generated by scripts/validate_panel_browser.cjs: desktop/mobile, appliances, Today, flow, home-balance and storage. Cards use fixed title/value/trend slots and two-line title limits. Appliance cards reserve energy/status/action areas. Today’s eight tiles, including weather, share the same height. Existing day/night scene and 10 W animation threshold remain.

## Checked behavior

Curves are replaced by a latest completed-quarter arrow and 24 directional samples across six hours. Missing history uses neutral horizontal arrows with explicit no-data labels, without fabricating power values. Arrows are cached until history, language or quarter changes. Header and value alignment is asserted across KPI cards.

All visible percentages have no decimals, including SOC, apparent daily ratios, network shares and weather probability; internal precision is preserved.

Appliance fixture verifies 1,524.81 kWh lifetime reading becomes 0.81 kWh for the day from a 1,524 kWh midnight baseline, with approximately EUR 0.17 at the configured tariff. No midnight baseline means no daily cost. Counter resets, units and 23/25-hour days have frontend unit coverage. Recorder queries are grouped and bounded; full-precision source readings prevent mirror rounding from creating false resets.

Weather fixture checks a daily forecast, local date, minimum/maximum, precipitation and integer probability through a read-only subscription. Unsupported forecasts fall back to explicitly labelled current conditions. Administrator configuration remains scoped.

Backend tests verify strict age blocking, observation age warnings without hiding a valid load balance, and rejection of missing/invalid sources. Battery stock confirmation bypasses only a misclassified cumulative state class; capacity and unit checks remain. Real HA scenarios exercise these paths through the configuration WebSocket and preserve entity IDs without calling device services.

## Limits

Physical sensors and the user’s unavailable-source cause cannot be confirmed from screenshots without the calculation diagnostic. Old reports are warnings in observation, not proof of fresh measurements. Counter differences are sampled-day estimates; a missing midnight reference cannot be reconstructed. A lifetime consumed-energy counter remains unsuitable for remaining battery stock even if its unit is kWh.
