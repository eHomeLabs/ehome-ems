# eHome EMS b11 — design QA

final result: passed

## Targets and scope

User-supplied reference cards: colored power/sparklines, battery SOC/stock and water heater temperature/day energy; central-house flow concept and Today summary. Compared with actual simulated panel captures at 1440×1600 and 390×844 CSS pixels, DPR 1. References contain different values and a different HA theme; the goal is their visual hierarchy inside the existing HA integration, not replacement of HA chrome or exact invented data.

Evidence generated by `node scripts/validate_panel_browser.cjs` in `artifacts/panel/`: desktop/mobile, flow, idle, storage, appliances, Today and two active batteries. Contact sheets `compare-flow.png`, `compare-metrics.png`, `compare-storage.png` reviewed with reference and implementation together. Cloud preview also inspected interactively for navigation and storage. The fixture uses document scrolling for full captures; the production panel retains its HA scroll container.

## Findings fixed and rechecked

- P1: storage fill misalignment when the icon exceeded its grid column. Both outline and measured fill now share an explicitly sized centered icon box. Fresh storage desktop/mobile captures confirm alignment.
- P2: large numeric power values wrapped before their unit in narrow KPI cards. Reduced responsive size, one-line values and raw-value titles; unavailable labels use smaller readable text. Fresh metric comparison confirms `1500 W` stays on one line.
- P2: old scene positions produced oversized source boxes, small house and disconnected links. Source tiles are compact, the house enlarged and centered, line endpoints calculated from actual bounds, and compact backup storage distinguished from connected active AC batteries. Two active battery cards stack without text overlap and outgoing connector extends through reserved scene space.
- P2: snapshot timing could observe geometry before ResizeObserver settled. Browser assertion now waits for the actual non-overlap condition rather than assuming the first frame is final.

## Five fidelity surfaces

- Typography: system/HA font, strong numeric hierarchy, smaller periods and estimates. No decorative font dependency. Visible missing states and source labels retained.
- Spacing/layout: top horizontal scrollable navigation, desktop flow plus strategy, mobile stacked sections; 44 px controls and keyboard source editing retained. Every new tab is reachable; no horizontal content overflow at tested widths.
- Colors/tokens: solar yellow, home blue, grid cyan, batteries green/red for low SOC, water heating amber, EV purple. Existing HA theme controls cards and navigation; source data remain readable without relying solely on color.
- Assets: existing local house WebP; HA's MDI battery/boiler icons for scalable indicators. Measured SOC and temperature are code-rendered data visualizations; no generated statistics or decorative substitute for the source house. Generic MDI silhouettes intentionally differ from the sample's custom rounded vessels.
- Copy/content: explicit day/session/total periods, measured remaining energy, estimates in EUR, no financial savings or solar provenance invented. No fabricated autonomy/self-consumption percentages. Battery runtime is indicative and qualified; heater fill is explicitly a temperature scale, not thermal energy.

## Interaction and data checks

127 Python tests and 10 JS tests pass locally. Browser suite passes long press / keyboard remapping, unavailable values, 10 W threshold (1 W remains visible without animation), reduced motion, independent copy, admin-only scenario persistence, appliance cost, Today, temperature, two batteries and all navigation. Real HA tests cover the new appliance/daily schema serialization, mirrors and settings persistence in addition to existing transport permissions/reload checks. No device commands are sent.

History comes from HA source history, grouped and throttled, with explicit missing-history state. Curves break across unknown values and long gaps. No recorded data means no invented curve. EUR equivalents require daily energy and an explicitly configured flat tariff; cumulative/session counters never become daily cost.

## Remaining P3 / intentional limits

No claim of pixel identity to a generated illustration. Generic icons, user HA theme and truthful missing states are intentional. Daily ratios and measured savings require a validated energy balance, not inferred from battery charge/discharge. Physical Android rendering and RPi4 fluidity remain for the user's hardware test.
